title: 'We are just programming ourselves'
description: 'This is what habits are'
pubDate: 'Sep 18 2026'
---

Habits are very important and instrumental to anyone's success in their pursuit of any opportunity.
What are habits then? I like Warren Buffet's quote that "The chains of habit are too weak to be felt until they are too strong to be broken".

I like to see habits as just as our internal programming and we program ourselves everyday much like you would program a computer.

Everytime you try to build a habit and do something you are attempting to change your behavior for the rest of your life.
If you had a habit of exercise, soon the habit of exercise would run so deep in you that it would be a part of you.

Habits are a way of lowering activation energy which is why they are so easy to follow after a long time, so then we really go through life making these habits and they are just ways to "program" ourselves, as if we are rewriting our code.

I think if I really want to change anything in my life, it would be then to modify my habits to those ends.
